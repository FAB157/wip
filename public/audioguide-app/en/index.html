The self-guided audio tour app that starts by itself as you walk

No tour to start, no numbers to key in: switch on the Guide, put your phone in your pocket and, when you reach a place, its story begins. On foot or by car, in 7 languages.

How the audio guide starts

  1. Put on your headphones and tap Guide in the bottom bar: the audio guide radar switches on.
  2. At about 150 m on foot (300 m by car) your phone vibrates: “You are approaching…”.
  3. When you arrive, at about 30 m on foot (50 m by car), a short voice teaser plays, free of charge.
  4. Right after, the full guide starts. In Semi-Automatic mode you tap Listen yourself.

Distances are measured along the road to the place’s arrival point, not as the crow flies, and you can change them in Profile › SETUP › Advanced GeoControl. The app tells walking from driving by your speed: above 12 km/h it switches to “car”, below 6 km/h back to “on foot”.

With the screen off, on foot and in the car

In the iPhone and Android app, a service on your phone keeps following your position even with the screen off, which is why it asks for location permission “Always”. The WipNav walking navigator also speaks with the screen off.

In the car the voice comes through the speakers your phone is connected to, like a sat nav. On the web (wip.guide in a browser) audio guides only work while the page is open in the foreground.

Which places speak

By default monuments and castles, museums and galleries, churches and places of worship speak; viewpoints, nature and tips you switch on in Setup. Gems, the places not to miss, always speak. A place you have already heard does not repeat for 24 hours.

Every guide is written from sources about that place (Wikipedia, Wikidata, its official website) and read by a synthetic voice: it is AI-generated content built on verified material. When there is no material about a place, no guide is made up.

Voices, languages and questions

Nicky, a female voice and the default; Dante, a male voice in classic-guide style; or the Duet, a two-voice dialogue. 7 languages: Italian, English, French, Spanish, German, Russian and Chinese. With Ask you pause and ask a question by voice or in writing; the in-depth option adds up to three more levels.

Museums, itineraries and offline

Museums

The Museum Visit takes you room by room and work by work, with audio, curiosities and, where available, the floor plan. Download it the night before: it works offline and stays yours with no expiry. With the Museum Pass you frame a work with the camera and listen to it (internet needed).

Itineraries

Ask for a trip of one or more days: stops are checked one by one, with walking distances, public transport for legs over 1.5 km and a printable PDF. The Library has ready-made itineraries, free.

Offline

In the app you download area packages and itineraries: map and audio guides work without a connection. Full guides offline need an active Day Pass or guides you already own; a guide never generated before needs the network.

How much it costs

The app is free to download and much of it is free to use: map, place cards, approach alerts and voice teasers, the walking navigator and the itinerary Library.

The full guide of a place costs 15 credits and stays yours forever. The Day Pass (200 credits) gives 24 hours of audio guides, up to 40 guides, offline and with the screen off too. No subscriptions: credits come in packs in the app and never expire. The store shows the price in your currency, which varies by country; the current price list is always in the app.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?

To get a guide the first time, yes. In the app you can download area packages and itineraries beforehand and listen offline; full guides offline need an active Day Pass or guides you already own.

Does it work with the screen off?

Yes, in the iPhone and Android app, with location permission set to “Always”. On the web only while the page is open.

How much does it cost?

Alerts and teasers are free. A full guide costs 15 credits and is yours forever; the Day Pass costs 200 credits for 24 hours, up to 40 guides. No subscriptions, and credits never expire.

Which languages does it speak?

Italian, English, French, Spanish, German, Russian and Chinese, with the voices of Nicky and Dante.

Do I have to do anything when I arrive?

No: in Automatic mode the guide starts by itself. If you prefer, Semi-Automatic mode alerts you and waits for you to tap Listen.

Who writes the guides?

They are generated with artificial intelligence from sources about that place (Wikipedia, Wikidata, the official website) and read by synthetic voices.

Can I try it without installing anything?

Yes, wip.guide opens in your browser. For audio guides with the screen off and offline you need the app.

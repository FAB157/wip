🎨 Street art: the WIP catalogue (987 places)

🎨 Street art — Murals, street art neighborhoods, open-air museums, free walls for graffiti, sculpture trails, and festival venues.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

What it is

Murals, street art neighborhoods, open-air museums, free walls for graffiti, sculpture trails, and festival venues. The app's catalogue contains 987 in 97 countries.

How to find it

  1. On the map tap ＋ More and turn on 🧭 Themed.
  2. In the themes row, tap 🎨 Street art. «All» turns them all on; tapping a theme from «All» leaves only that one.
  3. Zoom in on the area and tap a pin: the panel opens with a photo (if available), description, and the Guide, Navigate, and Audio buttons (reads the text aloud, upon request).
  4. Alternatively, open Itinerary › 🧭 Themed trips › Street art: list with search («Search among N places: name, city, country…»), filters 📍 Near me (200 km radius) and 🌍 Worldwide, and for each place «See on map» and «✨ Create itinerary».
  5. For a pre-written program, use Itinerary library › Themed › Street art.

What you read on the card

  • The type («Mural», «Street art neighborhood»…), city, and description. Artists and year are planned fields but are currently empty in the catalogue.
  • The map also shows thousands of murals imported from Wikidata and OpenStreetMap.

Themed card filters: Only search by name, city, or country.

Audio and photos

This theme does not have automatic audio (product decision): it has a card with a description and the «Audio» button that reads the text aloud when you ask. Photos are added after import and are not present for all places.

How the catalog is made

For individual works there are no photos by choice (non-uniform freedom of panorama). Street Art Cities is excluded due to its license. The catalog is curated by the editorial team and is updated by hand (there is no automatic update): an entry may sometimes be outdated. For this reason, each guide should be checked before setting off.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • No automatic audio for this theme.
  • On the map you see a sample of at most 400 places per tile: zoom in to see more.

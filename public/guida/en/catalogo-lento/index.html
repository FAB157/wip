🚂 Slow travel: the WIP catalog (233 places)

🚂 Slow travel — Scenic and historic trains, cable cars, funiculars, scenic ferries, cycle paths, canal boats and scenic roads.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

What it is

Scenic and historic trains, cable cars, funiculars, scenic ferries, cycle paths, canal boats and scenic roads. The app catalog contains 233 of them, in 57 countries.

How to find it

  1. On the map tap ＋ More and turn on 🧭 Themed.
  2. In the themes row tap 🚂 Slow travel. "All" turns them all on; tapping a theme from "All" leaves only that one.
  3. Zoom in on the area and tap a pin: the panel opens with a photo (if available), description, and the Guide, Navigate, and Audio buttons (reads the text aloud, upon request).
  4. Alternatively open Itinerary › 🧭 Themed trips › Slow travel: list with search ("Search among N places: name, city, country…"), filters 📍 Near me (200 km radius) and 🌍 Worldwide, and for each place "See on map" and "✨ Create itinerary".
  5. For a pre-written program use the Itineraries library › Themed › Slow travel.

What you read on the card

  • The type, city, description and, for all entries, the operator, the length in km and the duration ("🚉 operator · 📏 N km · ⏱ duration").
  • On the map you see the starting point; the routes are separate lines.

Themed card filters: Only search by name, city, or country.

Audio and photos

This theme does not have automatic audio (product decision): it has a card with a description and the «Audio» button that reads the text aloud when you ask. Photos are added after import and are not present for all places.

How the catalog is made

The route lines come from OpenStreetMap; the itinerary brief recalls duration, scenic side, advance booking and an alternative if the service is seasonal. The catalog is curated by the editorial team and updated by hand (there is no automatic update): an entry may no longer be up to date. For this reason, each card must be checked before leaving.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • No automatic audio for this theme.
  • On the map you see a sample of at most 400 places per tile: zoom in to see more.

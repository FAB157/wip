🎬 Film & series locations: the WIP catalog (1,416 places)

🎬 Film & series locations — Places where movies, series, and anime were filmed or are set, visitable studios, and cinema museums.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

What it is

Places where movies, series, and anime were filmed or are set, visitable studios, and cinema museums. The app catalog contains 1,416 of them in 78 countries.

How to find it

  1. On the map tap ＋ More and turn on 🧭 Themed.
  2. In the themes row, tap 🎬 Film & series locations. "All" turns them all on; tapping a theme from "All" leaves only that one.
  3. Zoom in on the area and tap a pin: the panel opens with a photo (if available), description, and the Guide, Navigate, and Audio buttons (reads the text aloud, upon request).
  4. Alternatively, open Itinerary › 🧭 Themed trips › Film & series locations: a list with search ("Search among N places: name, city, country..."), 📍 Near me filters (200 km radius) and 🌍 Worldwide, and for each place "See on map" and "✨ Create itinerary".
  5. For a pre-made program, use the Itinerary library › Themed › Film & series locations.

What you read on the card

  • In the full card, "🎬 Filmed here" (or Set here) appears with poster, director, actors, and the scene (from Wikipedia, CC BY-SA); "Show all" for more than 8 works.
  • In the catalog, up to 4 works per place: "🎞 title (year) — scene".
  • 🚪 "Visitable" badge for places open to the public.

Themed card filters: Only search by name, city, or country.

Audio and photos

This theme does not have automatic audio (product decision): it has a card with a description and the «Audio» button that reads the text aloud when you ask. Photos are added after import and are not present for all places.

How the catalog is made

The works are linked to the places via Wikidata (CC0 license); 475 locations without works were verified one by one and those without confirmation were discarded. IMDb or commercial atlases are not used. The catalogue is curated by the editorial team and is updated by hand (there is no automatic update): an entry may no longer be up‑to‑date. Therefore each card should be checked before you set off.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • No automatic audio for this theme.
  • On the map you see a sample of at most 400 places per tile: zoom in to see more.

Frequently asked questions

How many places are there in “Film & series locations”?

The curated catalogue contains 1,416 entries. The map may show more or fewer, because it reads the places archive.

Does it have an audio guide?

No: it has a card and an "Audio" button that reads the text aloud.

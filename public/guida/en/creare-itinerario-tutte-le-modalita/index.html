Create an itinerary: all modes and all options

The Itinerary ("Trip Planner") card has ten entry points. Here you find all, with steps, every field of the form, the default values and the cost. General rule: creation costs 10 credits per day (1–30 days) and a confirmation always appears before the charge.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

Automatic Mode: every field

  1. Open Itinerary › Automatic Mode.
  2. Optional: tap a seasonal inspiration (cards horizontal, 7 at a time, "Show more" for the others; chips: All, 💎 Hidden, 🏛 History, 🌊 Sea, ⛰ Mountain, 🎭 Culture, ✨ Uniqueness). The tap only fills destination, days, interests and requests, without generating and without cost.
  3. Fill out the form (table below).
  4. Tap "WIP, generate the itinerary" (active with at least 3 destination letters and connection).
  5. The credit confirmation appears: Confirm. If the destination isn’t found, it stops before charging.
  6. The itinerary appears piece by piece on the screen and then the result view opens.

Good to know. The choices are remembered for the next time. Below the button it says “If it’s already in our library, you get it immediately at half price”; in the app, if the itinerary is already in the library it shows “Found in library: no credit deducted”. In practice: if it already exists, it costs less or nothing. The library is used only if you haven’t specified accommodation, special requests or places to reel.

🧭 Special itineraries (under the module)

  • 🛳✈️ Short stop: ports and layovers, the best in the hours you have (dedicated page).
  • 🥾 Walks: stops and stamps already planned.
  • 🍷 Wine and taste routes: wineries, dairies and oil mills along the route.
  • 🧭 Thematic trips: spas, film sets, stars, street art, markets.
  • 🎭 Inspiration & themes: cinema, art, music, history (opens the filtered Library).

Talk to WIP

  1. Tap “Talk to WIP”.
  2. Write (e.g. “three days in Lisbon in October, no museums”) and tap Send, or use the microphone (“Listening…”).
  3. WIP asks one question at a time and reads the answer aloud (the switch “WIP speaks: on/off” remembers your choice).
  4. When it has destination and days it shows a summary with “Create the itinerary” and “Change something”.
  5. With “Create the itinerary” the data go into the form and normal generation starts, with its credit confirmation.
  • Collects: destination (multiple cities with “and”), days, month, interests, budget, pace, travelers, free only, times and special requests.
  • Cost: 3 credits per 10 messages (with confirmation) plus 10 credits per itinerary day. AI consent required. Maximum 20 exchanges.

📅 When to go

  1. Tap “When to go”.
  2. Type a city ("Type a city…") and tap OK.
  3. The card opens with two views, Year and Month.

Sections: Numbers, Overview, Web advice, Traveler experiences, Month by month, What to bring, Best times, Warnings, Bathing months, Recent years vs average, Sources. Buttons PDF, Listen, "Compare with another city". Data: NASA POWER averages 2001–2020. It’s free; AI analysis requires login.

From My Favorites

  1. Tap "From My Favorites" (if you have no places: "No saved gems yet" and the Explore map button).
  2. Check the places (counter "N Selected Items").
  3. Choose the period and the three checkboxes (Events, Tours, Free) and, if you want, the advanced options.
  4. Tap "OPTIMIZE ROUTE" (from 1 place).
  • Do not choose the days: they are the number of places divided by 3, rounded up (3 places = 1 day; 10 places = 4 days).
  • If the places are more than 150 km from the center, it asks if you want to continue.

Radius: 3 alternatives

  1. Tap "Radius".
  2. Choose the Starting Point: city, address or "Use my location".
  3. Choose the Radius (km): 100 / 300 / 500 or a custom value (1–5,000); default 300.
  4. Choose the interests: 🏛️ Art & Culture · 👨‍👩‍👧 Families · 🍷 Food & Wine · 🌊 Sea · ⛰️ Mountain · 🌳 Nature · 💆 Relaxation · 🧗 Adventure.
  5. Set duration (1–7 or "days" up to 30), period, times and the three checkboxes.
  6. Tap "Find 3 Alternatives" (free).
  7. Read the three proposals ("Here are some proposals for you"): title, description, days, stops. "Generate 3 more ideas" is free.
  8. Tap the proposal you like: a confirmation appears (10 × days) and the real generation starts.

Swip: swipe and choose

  1. Tap "Swip".
  2. Fill in: destination, days (1–7), period (needed for event cards), Attraction type (🏛️ Museums · 🗿 Monuments · ⛪ Churches · 📍 Attractions · 🍕 Gastronomy · 🌿 Nature · 🛍️ Shopping; no default selection).
  3. Tap "Start Swip!" (free).
  4. For each card: ❤ or swipe right to keep, X or swipe left to discard. Cancel returns to the previous one. "Read more / Read less" expands the text. At the top the counter N / total and the heart with the selected ones.
  5. With Events or Tours turned on, up to 5 "🎫 Real event" cards appear mixed in the deck.
  6. From 1 stop onward, tap "Review the Stops (N)": for each 🔄 Replace or ✕.
  7. Tap "Generate Itinerary with N stops": confirmation (10 × days) and generation.

Group trip

RoleSteps
Organizer (requires access)"Create a room" → choose **Destination**, **Days** (1–30), **Month** (optional), **Your name** → **"Create the room"** → get a **6‑digit PIN** → **"Invite the group"** (link wip.guide/?groupplan=PIN) → see "Voti arrivati: N" → **"Group merge"** → **"→ Apply to the form and generate →"**: the form is filled and **you generate**, paying the regular cost.
Who votes (no account)"I have a PIN" → enter the PIN → **"Enter the room"** → name; **What interests you?** (Art, Gastronomy, Nature, Adventure, Shopping, Photography); **Budget**; **Pace**; **Must‑see** and **Must‑avoid** (optional, 200 characters) → **"Send my preferences"**; then "Edit my vote".
  • Maximum 12 participants, room open 7 days, one vote per device (updates, does not duplicate).
  • Merge without AI: interests with at least 2 votes (or the top 3), median budget, most common pace; "must‑see" and "must‑avoid" go into special requests. Before paying, check everything: the merge only fills the form.
  • Room and votes are free; only the generator pays.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • There is no "Share itinerary" button with a public link for a single itinerary: you share via PDF, .ics calendar, story or Group trip.
  • "When to go" does not create an itinerary.
  • In "From My Favorites" days are not chosen.

Frequently asked questions

Which mode should I choose if I don’t know where to go?

"Radius" (3 free alternatives) or the seasonal inspirations in Automatic Mode.

Can I try without paying?

Yes: When to go, the 3 alternatives of the Radius, the Swip, the group room and the Library are free up to generation.

How many days at most?

30; with more cities the days are split among the cities.

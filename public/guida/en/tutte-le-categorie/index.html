All WIP categories, with numbers and usage

Each map place belongs to a category. This page lists all of them, grouped into families. For each you find the number of places visible in the app, where to turn it on on the map and whether it has an audio guide.

How to use this page

  1. Find the family you’re interested in.
  2. Read "Where to find it": it tells you which chip or layer to turn on on the map.
  3. Read "Audio guide": whether the place speaks for itself or not.
  4. In the table you see how many places there are for each category. "about" = database estimate.
  5. Open WIP, turn on the chip and tap a pin.
Good to know. A category without a number doesn’t have a reliable count right now: we don’t show it rather than make one up. The same category can appear in multiple ways on the map (for example a church can also be a gem).

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • The numbers count the places visible in the app for each category: a place counts only once, in its main category.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn’t the sum match the total?

Some places have categories that aren’t listed or are newer, and the large totals are estimates.

Where do I find a category that I don’t see in the bar?

Add it from + More, or turn on the right layer from Layers.

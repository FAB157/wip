WIP › Guide › The interface

The WIP interface explained: bar, chips, buttons

A visual guide to the controls: where everything is and what it does when you tap it.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

The bottom bar

EntryWhat it does
ExploreThe map with places and levels.
ItineraryCreate, open, and download itineraries; Library; group travel.
EventsEvents, exhibitions, and seasonal content.
Camera buttonVision: recognizes places, artworks, nature; AR Radar; Museum visits.
WIP AIChat with the assistant (3 credits every 10 messages).
GuideTurns the audio guide radar on or off; the button above mutes it.
ProfileSettings, credits, Shop, price list, archive, manual, group tour.

At the top of the map

  • Category chips: Gems, Monuments, WIP Community, Experiences, Events; use ＋ More to add Nature, Places, Venues, Utilities, Families, Cultural Heritage, Themed.
  • Sub-chips: these appear below the bar when the chip is turned on and refine your search (for example Churches, Museums, Viewpoints).

On the map

  • Layers (bottom left): trails, cycle paths, wine and food, snow, sun, climate, sea quality, satellite, and more.
  • Lens 🔍: search for a city.
  • Crosshairs: centers on your location; hold down to make the map follow you.
  • Weather indicator at the bottom center: temperature and, if it is raining, an alert.
  • Numbered pins: multiple nearby places grouped together; zoom in to separate them.

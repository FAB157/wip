Museums, monuments and culture with WIP

For cultural sites, WIP offers profiles, audio guides and, for the most important museums, a guided room-by-room Visit. Here's where to find them and how they work.

Finding a cultural site

  1. Search for the city with the magnifying glass 🔍.
  2. Turn on 🏛️ Monuments or 💎 Gems: below the bar choose Monuments, Churches, Museums, Panoramas.
  3. Tap a pin and open the profile: description, photos, technical details and audio guide player.

Gems are the most important sites and always speak. The 🏺 Cultural Heritage chip (from ＋ More) is the atlas of protected assets: it provides the profile but no audio guide.

The Museum Visit

In the Camera profile, Visits section, for museums that offer it you'll find the Museum Visit: a room-by-room path, ordered by proximity, with the «You are here / Next» bar and the voice that notifies you at the end of each room.

  • For each artwork: explanation, curiosities, photos, comparisons and three ready-to-use questions that work offline.
  • The museum floor plan with pins, where available: the number on the pin indicates the artworks in that room.
  • Export to PDF with cover, photos and floor plan.
  • The voice files (MP3) are downloaded before the visit and remain in the «Museum Guides» downloads folder.
Cost. Museum Visit: 200 credits per museum, no expiration, with 20 scans included. Museum Pass: 100 credits for 40 scans in 4 hours. The guides are generated from verified sources (Wikipedia, museum website, open archives).

Scanning an artwork

In the Camera, AI Scan section, select 🖼️ Artwork, frame it and receive recognition with explanation. Each scan costs 5 credits; offline the photos are recognized and charged when you go back online.

Tickets

Tickets for museums and attractions are booked through partners (Tiqets, GetYourGuide, Viator) from the Experiences chip or from the card; purchase takes place on the partner's website. WIP receives a commission without raising the price.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • The map with pins isn’t available for all museums: where it’s missing, the guide is a list of artworks.
  • Not all museums have a guided tour.

Frequently asked questions

Are museum guides available in multiple languages?

The guide is prepared in Italian and translated into the other app languages where available.

Does it work indoors?

The Museum Pass isn’t location‑dependent, so it also works inside the museum; it requires internet.

🛍️ Markets & fairs: the WIP catalog (810 places)

🛍️ Markets & fairs — Christmas markets, flea and antique markets, food and craft markets, night markets, floating markets, and historic indoor markets.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

What it is

Christmas markets, flea and antique markets, food and craft markets, night markets, floating markets, and historic indoor markets. The app's catalog contains 810 of them, across 73 countries.

How to find it

  1. On the map tap ＋ More and turn on 🧭 Themed.
  2. In the themes row, tap 🛍️ Markets & fairs. "All" turns them all on; tapping a theme from "All" leaves only that one.
  3. Zoom in on the area and tap a pin: the panel opens with a photo (if available), description, and the Guide, Navigate, and Audio buttons (reads the text aloud, upon request).
  4. Alternatively, open Itinerary › 🧭 Themed trips › Markets & fairs: list with search ("Search among N places: name, city, country…"), 📍 Near me filters (200 km radius) and 🌍 Worldwide, and for each place "See on map" and "✨ Create itinerary".
  5. For a pre-made schedule, use the Itineraries library › Themed › Markets & fairs.

What you read on the card

  • The type, city, description, and best period ("best time") in an amber box.
  • In Events › Seasonal: "🛍️ Open markets now", designed for Christmas markets (November 15 – January 6).

Themed guide filters: In the "Themed itineraries" tab: "📅 In season only" (turned on by default).

Audio and photos

This theme does not have automatic audio (product decision): it has a card with a description and the «Audio» button that reads the text aloud when you ask. Photos are added after import and are not present for all places.

How the catalog is made

OpenStreetMap markets (markets, covered markets, farmers' markets, fleas) end up in this vertical rather than under Utilities. The catalog is curated by the editorial team and is updated manually (there is no automatic update): an entry may no longer be up to date. For this reason, each card should be verified before leaving.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • No automatic audio for this theme.
  • The current catalog is missing the opening months: «Seasonal only» rules out nothing and the «Markets open now» section may appear empty.
  • There is no real event calendar for the markets.
  • On the map you see a sample of at most 400 places per tile: zoom in to see more.

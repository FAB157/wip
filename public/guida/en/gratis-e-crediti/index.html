What is free and what uses credits in WIP

WIP works with credits: those needed for search and viewing are free, those that require content generation or external services cost credits. Credits do not expire.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

Free

  • Map, layers and place cards (descriptions, photos, opening hours).
  • WipNav on foot.
  • Proximity alerts and teaser voice messages.
  • Itinerary Library and offline maps.
  • WIP Community.
  • Events and Exhibitions.
  • Diary and Missions.
  • Audio guides "Tips".

The updated price list is always in the app: Profile › List.

How to get credits

  • Purchase in Profile › Shop (City-Break, Week, Tour Operator packages). The price in euros is the one shown by the store or the payment; it is not fixed on this page.
  • Earning them: Missions, experience points and upload quizzes (earned ones are consumed first).
  • With a coupon in Profile › SETUP › "Have a promo coupon?" (not available on iPhone).

Payments: on the native app via store, on the web with Stripe. With the Rain guarantee you can recover the credits for a one‑day itinerary if it rained heavily.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • There are no subscriptions: only credit packages and Day Pass.

Frequently asked questions

Do credits expire?

No.

Can I use WIP without spending?

Yes: map, cards, WipNav on foot, teaser, Itinerary Library and Events are free.

How much does a package cost?

The price is shown by the store (or the web payment): it varies by country.

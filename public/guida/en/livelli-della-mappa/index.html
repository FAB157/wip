The 12 WIP map layers: what they show and how to use them

The Layers button (bottom left) turns on information layers above the map. There are 12, in three groups. Each has a guide with what it draws, at what zoom it appears, what you read by tapping, where the data comes from and its limits.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

How the panel works

  1. On the map, tap the circle with the layers («Map layers») at the bottom left.
  2. Find the group («Where to go», «How it is now» or «Background») and tap the layer row: the row colors and a ✓ appears (a spinner while loading).
  3. Close the panel: next to the button, the emojis of the active layers and a dot with the number remain. Tap them to reopen.
  4. To turn it off, tap the row again, or «Turn off all (n)».
  • The layers add up and your choice is remembered between sessions: an active layer restarts on its own when reopened.
  • «↗ zoom in to see them»: the layer is on but you are too far away.
  • If you have an open info card, the layer does not reload until you close it.
  • Layers do not have an audio guide: they show, they don't tell.

When you need several together

  • Bike ride: 🚲 Bike paths + 🚰 Drinking fountains + ☀️ Sun.
  • Hike: 🥾 Trails + ☀️ Sun + 📅 Climate + 🌿 Protected areas.
  • Snow weekend: ❄️ Snow + ☀️ Sun.
  • Beach day: 🏖 Water quality + ☀️ Sun.
  • Tasting tour: 🍷 Wine and food + 🚲 Bike paths.
  • To see from above: 🛰️ Satellite underneath all the others.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • No layer has internal filters.
  • Webcams, tides and Blue Flag do not exist; ZTL traffic zones are no longer available.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the layer on but I don't see anything?

You are probably too far away: zoom in on the area («zoom in to see them»).

Do the layers stay on?

Yes: the app remembers which ones you have turned on.

How to find gluten-free restaurants with WIP

WIP has a dedicated Gluten-Free filter with four types of venue and two levels of declared guarantee. This page explains how to read them and where the data limit lies.

How the filter works

  • Levels: "100% senza glutine" (the venue declares it is completely gluten-free) and "Con menu" (it has a gluten-free menu alongside the regular one).
  • Pin tags: "100%" (completely gluten-free), "Menu" (gluten-free menu, but the kitchen also uses gluten) and "Opz." (reported by users, no dedicated menu: ask before ordering). In the center of the pin is the venue type.
  • Types: Pizzerias, Gelaterias, Bakeries.
  • The levels and types add up: 100% + gelaterias shows you the gelaterias that are completely gluten-free.
  1. Turn on 🍕 Places from + More.
  2. Tap 🌾 Gluten-Free.
  3. Choose level and type; tap ℹ️ Legend for definitions.
  4. Open a pin and check the card: "Gluten-Free Available".

What it means, and what it doesn’t

Good to know. The filter is built on declared tags (OpenStreetMap and venue cards). It means "the venue appears gluten-free", not "certified for celiacs". Menus and procedures change: before you sit down, call (the number is on the card).

The number at the top of the page counts the venues with the tag; it should not be read as a list of venues verified one by one.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • WIP does not verify AIC certifications nor the absence of contamination.

Frequently asked questions

Does the filter include gelaterias and bakeries?

Yes: among the types are Pizzerias, Gelaterias and Bakeries.

Where can I read the difference between 100% and With menu?

Tap "ℹ️ Legend" in the Gluten-Free filter row.

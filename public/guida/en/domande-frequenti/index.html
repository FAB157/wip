Frequently asked questions about WIP

The answers are collected from all the guide pages. Use the search below to find the right page.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

Frequently asked questions

Map and search

Can I use WIP without signing up?

The website opens and shows the map. Some features (credits, itineraries, purchases) require an account. The free features are listed on the “What’s free” page.

How do I search for a specific place?

Search the city with the magnifying glass, then turn on the right category with the chips and tap the pin. Searching for individual places by name isn’t available.

Categories catalog

Why do some numbers say "about"?

For very large tables the database provides an estimate instead of an exact count. When this happens, the number is marked "about".

What are Gems?

They are places flagged as particularly important: they always play with the audio guide, even if their category is turned off in the settings.

Restaurants and gastronomy

Can I trust gluten‑free restaurants for celiac disease?

WIP shows venues that declare the diet. It does not verify certifications or kitchen procedures: always contact the venue.

Can I filter by cuisine or by vegan?

No. The real filters are the ones listed above. Cuisine is information on the venue’s card.

How do I find the nearest bar or ice‑cream shop?

Turn on Places and choose "Bar & Café" or "Gelati": the map shows only those.

Gluten-Free Restaurants

Does the filter include gelaterias and bakeries?

Yes: among the types are Pizzerias, Gelaterias and Bakeries.

Where can I read the difference between 100% and With menu?

Tap "ℹ️ Legend" in the Gluten-Free filter row.

Starred Restaurants

What does Bib mean?

It is the "Bib Gourmand" distinction from the Michelin guide. The app’s legend explains it next to the stars.

Does WIP show menu prices?

No, by design: the card links to the official website.

Wine & Taste

Are wine routes drawn?

The layer shows taste routes and shops; the detail depends on the data available in the area.

Do I need to zoom to see the wineries?

Yes, some elements appear only at certain zoom levels to keep the map light.

Nature and routes

Can I download a GPX file?

No, it’s not available.

Can I find elevation gain and difficulty?

No: the layer shows the track, not the altimetric data.

Are cycle routes only Italian?

The level includes European (EuroVelo) and national networks.

Trails and cycle routes

Can I follow a trail with voice guidance?

WipNav guides you on foot along road routes; for trails the level is used to see the track.

Sea and beaches

Is the sea temperature in real time?

It's a model data point, referring to the area: read it as an indication.

Are there tides?

No, they are not available in the app.

Weather and snow

Can I see the webcam of the slopes?

No, WIP doesn't have webcams.

Are the forecasts in the itinerary?

Yes: with the date set, the itinerary shows the forecast rain and Plan B.

Museums and culture

Are museum guides available in multiple languages?

The guide is prepared in Italian and translated into the other app languages where available.

Does it work indoors?

The Museum Pass isn’t location‑dependent, so it also works inside the museum; it requires internet.

Audio guides

Can I change voice and language?

Yes: the voice (Nicky, Dante, or Duet) from the player or Setup, the language from the Profile settings.

Does the teaser cost anything?

No, the notice and teaser are free.

Does it work offline?

Yes, in the app, with downloaded area packages and, for full guides, an active Day Pass or guides already purchased.

Why doesn’t a guide start?

Check that the category is enabled in Setup, that the GPS is accurate (under 50 m), and that the location hasn’t already been narrated in the last 24 hours.

Itineraries

How much does an itinerary cost?

10 credits per day. The Ready Itinerary Library is free.

Can I have only free things?

Yes: the «Free» option keeps only free stops (meals excluded).

Can I share it?

Yes: PDF, guide sending, travel story and Group Trip with PIN.

Itinerary Library

Is the Library really free?

Yes, viewing and saving itineraries costs no credits.

Navigator

Does WipNav work with the screen off?

In the iPhone and Android app, yes; on the web, no.

Can I navigate by car?

Use the button to open Google Maps (or Apple Maps on iPhone).

Events

Are the events free?

Viewing the list is free; the ticket depends on the event. There is the "Free only" filter.

What are the seasonal ones?

Period‑related events: starry skies, markets, blooms.

Useful services

Can I find a parking spot?

There is no dedicated chip for parking.

Can I find charging stations?

Yes: among the Utility filters there is "EV Charging".

Free and credits

Do credits expire?

No.

Can I use WIP without spending?

Yes: map, cards, WipNav on foot, teaser, Itinerary Library and Events are free.

How much does a package cost?

The price is shown by the store (or the web payment): it varies by country.

Web, iPhone and Android

Do I need to install something to try it?

No: wip.guide opens in the browser.

Why is the “Always” location permission needed?

To start the audio guides when you arrive, even with the screen off.

Tutorial

Do I need to register to follow the tutorials?

No. Progress is saved only in your browser.

Free audio guide

What happens if I close the warning card?

That place won't reappear for 30 minutes.

How do I mute everything?

With the round 🔊/🔇 button above the headphones: it also silences WipNav turns.

Why did the card open but not speak?

You are in Semi‑Automatic (tap Listen) or in silent mode.

Tour with audio guide

Does creating a tour cost anything?

No. A Day Pass is only needed to start navigation of a multi-stop tour.

Can I change a stop while walking?

Yes: remove it with the X on the map (WIP suggests a replacement) or use "Skip this stop".

Does closing the blue card stop the tour?

No: the tour only stops from the dashboard with the X.

WipNav step by step

Does WipNav cost credits?

No, it’s free, even within a tour.

Can I change when it notifies me?

No: the warnings are automatic. You can change the arrival distance in GeoControl.

Does it work in a car?

No: for the car use Google Maps (or Maps on iPhone) from the "Navigate" sheet.

Create an itinerary

Which mode should I choose if I don’t know where to go?

"Radius" (3 free alternatives) or the seasonal inspirations in Automatic Mode.

Can I try without paying?

Yes: When to go, the 3 alternatives of the Radius, the Swip, the group room and the Library are free up to generation.

How many days at most?

30; with more cities the days are split among the cities.

Short stop

If I’m wrong and return late?

WIP can’t guarantee the return: it calculates the margin but you always check the ship or flight times.

Is it free?

Consulting the catalog and previews is free; generating the itinerary costs the equivalent of one day (10 credits).

Edit an itinerary

How many free substitutions do I have?

3 per day; then 8 credits each.

If I move a stop does the time change?

No: the positions keep their time.

All categories

Why doesn’t the sum match the total?

Some places have categories that aren’t listed or are newer, and the large totals are estimates.

Where do I find a category that I don’t see in the bar?

Add it from + More, or turn on the right layer from Layers.

Beni Culturali

Why don’t I see Cultural Heritage?

You need to add them from + More and zoom in enough.

Do they have an audio guide?

No, only the card.

Custom route

Can I change the places after paying?

Yes, up to 3 changes; then you must create a new route.

If I finish the route, will I get the credits back?

No: credits already spent are not refunded.

Does it work for the car?

The WIP navigator is on foot; with the "All stops in Google Maps (car)" button you open the stops in Google Maps.

Thematic maps

Why don’t I see a layer turned on?

You’re probably too far away: «zoom in to see them» appears. Zoom in on the area.

How do I remove everything?

Layers › "Turn off all".

Why doesn’t Thematic show the theme row?

It must be in the bar and turned on.

How to buy

How much does a package cost in euros?

The store shows it at the time of purchase: it depends on the country and may change.

Do credits expire?

No.

What happens when they run out?

Paid actions prompt you to top up; free features continue to work.

Vision

How much does a scan cost?

5 credits; if it doesn't recognize anything, the credits are refunded.

Can I use a photo already in the gallery?

Yes: tap «Scegli dalla Galleria»; if it lacks a location, it asks you where you took it.

Does it work without the internet?

The photo is queued and recognized when you are back online.

Museum guide

Can I use the guide without the internet?

Yes, if you tapped "Download all" beforehand. Only the custom question requires a network.

What happens if I don't need the Visit in that museum?

Under 12 works it is not sold; in that case you scan the works one by one.

How do I tell WIP which room I'm in?

Scan the number above the door ("Where am I?") or tap "I'm here" in the room.

Group tour

How much does it cost for followers?

Nothing: only the leader pays, for the audio guides they start.

Does everyone need to have the app?

The wip.guide website in a browser or the app is enough; followers don't even need to register.

Podcast and Author Guide

Does the podcast use a real voice?

It uses your device's system voice in the app's language.

Do I lose credits if the guide doesn't arrive?

No: if generation fails, the server refunds.

Can I have the guide in another language?

Yes, with «Translate into…» at half price.

Profile and Setup

How do I change the language?

Profile › SETUP › System Language.

Where do I enter a coupon?

Only in Profile › SETUP › «Have a promo coupon?» (not on iPhone).

How do I delete my account?

At the bottom of SETUP: «Delete Account», type «delete» and confirm. It is irreversible.

Archive, downloads and points

How do I earn credits?

With quizzes (1 credit per correct answer), Missions, level rewards, and approved photos in the WIP Community.

Where can I find a guide I've already listened to?

In Profile › ARCHIVE: «Listen again (already purchased)».

What happens if I don't claim a reward?

It stays pending until you tap «CLAIM REWARDS».

First 5 minutes

Do I need to create an account?

To view the map and details, no; credits, itineraries, and purchases require an account.

Troubleshooting

Is GPS always inaccurate in the city?

Events step by step

Does «Perfect evening» cost credits?

Why am I seeing events from another city?

Borghi and villages

How do I know if a borgo has an audio guide?

If it's a gem 💎: you'll find the player in the card.

Why don't I hear anything when passing through a town?

Only gem borghi talk; the others have a card, but remain silent.

Map layers

Do the layers stay on?

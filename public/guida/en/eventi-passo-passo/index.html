The Events tab step by step: active travel, Perfect evening, filters

The Events tab (third item on the bottom bar) gathers food festivals, exhibitions, concerts, markets and tours around you or your travel destination. Here is every element of the screen, from top to bottom.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

Tutorial: finding what to do tonight

  1. Open Events from the bottom bar.
  2. Choose where: with an active travel tap "Events in {città}… (active travel)"; otherwise "Current position" or "Search city…".
  3. Look at "🌙 Tonight near you": tonight's closest events.
  4. Want a whole program? Tap "🍸 Perfect evening in one tap".
  5. For other days set FROM and TO at the bottom (default: today and one month from today).
  6. Widen the Distance to 300 or 500 km if you are looking for something further away, or turn on 🆓 Free only.
  7. Tap a card: Navigate to go there, or the partner link for the ticket.

🍸 Perfect evening in one tap

  1. Tap "Perfect evening in one tap". Access is required and, the first time, consent to the use of AI (the location is sent to an external model).
  2. Wait «Preparing your evening…» (up to 45 seconds).
  3. The proposal opens: the evening's stops with estimated budget, «Open in map» and «Tickets» where available.
  • It is composed only of real venues and events in the area. If it doesn't find a real venue within 25 miles, it says: «I can't find any real venues within 25 miles of this point: search for a location above and try again.» — it doesn't make anything up.
  • The proposal applies to the area: if you change city, it regenerates.
  • At the bottom: «Proposal generated with AI based on real local venues and events: check times and availability.»

Event sources

Local portals and Virgilio (Italy), Ticketmaster, Viator, GetYourGuide, Tiqets, Klook, Trip.com, local food festivals and markets, exhibitions from museum websites. Partners are shown first («by relevance» order); you can sort by date. You book on the partner's website: the price doesn't change for you, WIP receives a commission.

Exhibitions & Seasonal

  • 🖼️ Exhibitions › Current: temporary exhibitions from museum websites, Wikidata and Tiqets; Permanent: local museum collections, with «Open in map».
  • 🌸 Seasonal: «🌌 Tonight under the stars» (cloud cover, moon phase, meteor showers), «🛍️ Markets open now», «🌸 Blossoms in progress» and «⏳ Coming soon». Tapping a row opens the map on the location.
  • The Distance applies here too: with 300 mi you see exhibitions, collections and seasonal events up to 300 mi away.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • Small areas have few events: widen the distance.
  • Events in the Seasonal section depend on catalog season data, which for markets and blossoms are incomplete.
  • Ticketmaster does not cover Japan.

Frequently asked questions

Does «Perfect evening» cost credits?

There is no credit confirmation in the app: login and AI consent are required.

Why am I seeing events from another city?

You have an active trip: tap «Current position» to go back to where you are.

Vision: recognize places, artworks, and nature with a photo

The circular camera button, centered in the bottom bar, opens "Analyze the World": you frame something and WIP recognizes it and tells you about it like an audio guide. Each scan costs 5 credits.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

In short

  • Three tabs: AI Scan, AR Radar, Visits (museums: see the dedicated page).
  • Three scanning modes: 📍 Place, 🖼️ Artwork, 🌿 Nature.
  • 5 credits per scan; with an active Museum Pass the scan is included.
  • If nothing is recognized, the credits are refunded and the photo remains in My Vision.
  • First time: consent for the use of artificial intelligence is required.

The three modes of AI Scan

ModeWhat to frameWhat you get
📍 Place (default)A monument, a church, a building, a point of interest.The place card, saved in My Vision. Gives 10 XP.
🖼️ ArtworkA painting, a statue, an artifact.Recognition of the artwork with a narration by an art historian. If you are in a museum, its guided tour also starts in the background.
🌿 NatureA plant, an animal, a landscape.A naturalist card with habitat and curiosities.
Good to know. There is also a “Screenshot” mode in the code (from reel to itinerary), but not reachable in the app: it is unavailable.

Tutorial: recognizing a place

  1. Tap the circular camera button in the bottom bar. "Analyze the World" opens on the AI Scan tab.
  2. Choose the mode (Place, Artwork, or Nature): a sentence under the title explains what it does.
  3. First time: accept the AI consent request. Without consent the photo does not leave the phone.
  4. Tap "Take Photo" (rear camera in full-screen: white circle to capture, X to close) or "Choose from Gallery".
  5. The confirmation box appears with the cost (5 credits) and your balance: confirm it. With an active Museum Pass it does not appear.
  6. During analysis you see the photo with the scanning line.
  7. The place card opens: name, description, history, curiosities. The circular button "Listen to the card" reads it aloud.

Gallery photo: where did you take it?

  • If the photo already has a location (GPS in the photo data), WIP uses it and says: «Location and date read from the photo».
  • If it doesn't, «Where did you take this photo?» appears: tap the map to place the pin and then «Confirm location»; or «Use my current location»; or «I don't know» to analyze without coordinates.
  • A photo from the gallery never uses your phone's current GPS.

When it's not sure, or doesn't recognize

  • «I'm not sure: which one is it?»: up to 3 candidates; choose the right one and the card is rewritten for free. Or «Keep the card as is».
  • «I didn't recognize it… but it's definitely special!»: credits are refunded and the photo is saved. Choose the tags (Special panorama, Hidden corner, Street art, Local tradition, Nature, Travel memory) and, if you want, write a story (up to 2,000 characters). «Skip» or «Send your story».
  • In a museum tour, for artworks «Is it one of these?» appears with photos of the works in the room.
  • If there are recognizable faces in the photo, they will be blurred if published.

Without network and limits

  • Offline, the photo goes into a queue and is recognized when you return online; you don't pay until it is processed.
  • Queue: maximum 10 photos, valid for 7 days, 3 attempts per photo.
  • If you see «Too many unrecognized photos today», it's an anti-spam protection: try again tomorrow with a well-framed subject.

My Vision and WIP Community

Each scan creates a card in Profile › My Vision, with two views: «My Visions» and «WIP Community» («Near me» chip, 50 km radius, and «Most recent»).

StatusMeaning
Under reviewThe photo is being checked.
WIP CommunityApproved and published on the map with your attribution: you earn credits.
Not publishedRejected; see the «Reason» (place already present, not a point of interest, insufficient quality, recognizable people, inappropriate content, other).
First discovererYou are the first one to find it.

There's no «Propose» button: a recognized photo goes into review automatically. An automatic check discards people, screens, food, and places already present within a few meters; then a human approves. The welcome message mentions «up to +15 credits»: the actual amount per photo isn't stated here. The photos remain yours.

What to photograph: roadside shrines, murals, fountains, public washhouses, bridges, towers, boundary stones, minor churches, viewpoints. What not to: private interiors, recognizable people, copyrighted works as the main subject, commercial signs.

AR radar: the places around you, in front of the camera

  • Show places within 5 km, up to 12 labels in your field of view, with a photo or icon, name, and distance. Tap a label to open the card.
  • 2D radar («Lista e radar 2D»): 2 km radius, north at the top, the 24 closest to you, with the «I più vicini» list. Places with ready text show «audioguida pronta».
  • On iPhone, «Attiva la Bussola» appears: «Consenti» or «Salta (mostra tutti i POI)».
  • If camera permission is denied, the radar still works («Riprova» to grant permission again).
  • It is free and there are no options to change the radius or categories.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • The «Screenshot / reel to itinerary» mode is not available.
  • The AR radar has no settings.
  • A different cost for «Chiedi di più» on the card is not provided.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a scan cost?

5 credits; if it doesn't recognize anything, the credits are refunded.

Can I use a photo already in the gallery?

Yes: tap «Scegli dalla Galleria»; if it lacks a location, it asks you where you took it.

Does it work without the internet?

The photo is queued and recognized when you are back online.

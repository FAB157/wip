Discover everything you can do with WIP

WIP is a guide that accompanies you while you walk or drive: it shows the places around you on a map and, when you arrive, tells their story with a voice. This help center explains, feature by feature, what you can do, how to do it and how much it costs.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

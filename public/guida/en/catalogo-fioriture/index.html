What it is

Cherry blossoms, lavender, tulips, wisteria, sunflowers, autumn foliage, almond trees, rhododendrons, wildflowers, and botanical gardens. The app's catalog contains 476, in 43 countries.

How to find it

  1. On the map tap ＋ More and turn on 🧭 Themed.
  2. In the theme row, tap 🌸 Blossoms & blooms. «All» turns them all on; tapping a theme from «All» leaves only that one.
  3. Zoom in on the area and tap a pin: the panel opens with a photo (if available), description, and the Guide, Navigate, and Audio buttons (reads the text aloud, upon request).
  4. Alternatively, open Itineraries › 🧭 Themed trips › Blossoms & blooms: list with search («Search among N places: name, city, country…»), filters 📍 Near me (200 km radius) and 🌍 Worldwide, and for each place «See on map» and «✨ Create itinerary».
  5. For a pre-written program, use Itinerary library › Themed › Blossoms & blooms.

What you read on the card

  • The type, city, description, and best time to visit («March 25 – April 15, early morning»).
  • In Events › Seasonal: «🌸 Blossoms & blooms in progress» and «⏳ Coming soon».

Themed card filters: In the «Themed itineraries» card: «📅 Seasonal only».

Audio and photos

This theme does not have automatic audio (product decision): it has a card with a description and the «Audio» button that reads the text aloud when you ask. Photos are added after import and are not present for all places.

How the catalog is made

Blooms may shift by 10–15 days: always check. Do not enter cultivated fields. The catalog is curated by the editorial staff and updated manually (there is no automatic update): an entry may occasionally be outdated. For this reason, every guide should be verified before leaving.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • No automatic audio for this theme.
  • In the current catalog, structured months are missing: «On now» and «Coming soon» may appear empty; the period remains in the text.
  • There is no live source for blooms.
  • On the map you see a sample of at most 400 places per tile: zoom in to see more.

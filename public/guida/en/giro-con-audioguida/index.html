The Tour with audio guide: Radar, stops, dashboard and Day Pass

The Tour is the audio guide with a route: choose up to 10 places, WIP orders them, guides you with voice and at each stop the audio guide starts. Creating it is free; navigating requires the Day Pass.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

In short

  • It is created from the Radar Audioguida (headphones button on the map) or from a day of an itinerary with \"Nel radar (N)\".
  • Up to 10 stops (\"Full tour: ten stops maximum\").
  • Create the tour and preview it: free. \"Start navigation\": requires the Day Pass (200 credits).
  • For a single stop, just Navigate, free.

Tutorial: create your tour

  1. Turn on GUIDA and tap the headphones button on the map: the Radar Audioguida (\"N monitored places\") opens.
  2. For each place you want, tap +: it becomes the order number (the same as the pin). Tapping the number removes it. The trash can removes the place from the radar.
  3. Choose \"Time you have\": 30′, 1 h, 1 h 30, 2 h, 3 h, 4 h or All. WIP keeps the stops that fit (walking plus about 3 minutes of listening per stop) and says \"In the time you have there are N stops out of M\".
  4. Choose \"Finish\": 🔁 Return to where I start · Return to where I am · 🏁 End at the last stop · 🗺️ Only the tour, from the 1st stop (useful from home).
  5. Check the preview (\"12.3 km · 45 min on foot · loop from where you are\"). Above 8 km a warning appears about the long walk.
  6. Optional, order: WIP arranges the stops in the order that requires the least walking; drag ⠿ to change it, X to remove, \"Reorder for me\" to revert to automatic order.
  7. Optional, \"Along the way · N places the tour passes by\": tap the green + to add them. You'll hear the others as you pass, if they are within 40 m.
  8. Tap \"Create the tour\" (or \"Create the tour (N)\"). Status: \"Ready to go\".
  9. Tap \"Start navigation\". Without a Day Pass a prompt opens: \"Buy now\". As soon as it's activated, the tour starts on its own.

From a day of an itinerary

In the header of each day tap \"Nel radar (N)\": brings the day's stops (maximum 10) into the Radar. Restaurants, breaks and transfers remain mute stops: only \"You have arrived at …\" is announced, without audio guide or costs.

StatusWhenWhat’s happening

At the entranceAt "Guidance Start Distance" (30 m default)The audio guide starts and the card opens. If it doesn’t open, the tour opens it after 8 seconds.

FinishedLast stop reached"Tour complete": Save to My itineraries, Share, New tour from here.
  • Skimmed stop: you approached within 60 m and moved away by over 40 m: it counts as completed.
  • Encounters along the way: a radar place within 40 m of the route and you receive a teaser ("On your way"), once per tour.
  • X on the map: if you remove a stop, WIP suggests a replacement within 250 m ("Put it in its place?" Yes/No, expires in 2 minutes).
  • Two stops within 60 m count as one pause.
  • Preload: "Preparing stops for offline…" downloads text and audio; "Retry" if any are missing.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • A multi-stop tour cannot be navigated without a Day Pass (preview is allowed).
  • The tour’s warning and deviation thresholds cannot be changed; you can change the warning and arrival distances in GeoControl.

Frequently asked questions

Does creating a tour cost anything?

No. A Day Pass is only needed to start navigation of a multi-stop tour.

Can I change a stop while walking?

Yes: remove it with the X on the map (WIP suggests a replacement) or use "Skip this stop".

Does closing the blue card stop the tour?

No: the tour only stops from the dashboard with the X.

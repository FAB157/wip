🌌 Dark skies & stargazing: the WIP catalogue (895 places)

🌌 Dark skies & stargazing — Parks, reserves and dark‑sky communities, spots for stargazing, observatories, planetariums, aurora viewing points and astronomical villages.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

What it is

Parks, reserves and dark sky communities, stargazing spots, observatories, planetariums, aurora spots and astronomical villages. The app catalog features 895 of them in 101 countries.

How to find it

  1. On the map tap ＋ More and turn on 🧭 Themed.
  2. In the themes row tap 🌌 Dark skies & stars. "All" turns them all on; tapping a theme from "All" leaves only that one.
  3. Zoom in on the area and tap a pin: the panel opens with a photo (if available), description, and the Guide, Navigate, and Audio buttons (reads the text aloud, upon request).
  4. Alternatively open Itinerary › 🧭 Themed trips › Dark skies & stars: list with search ("Search among N places: name, city, country…"), 📍 Near me filters (200 km radius) and 🌍 Worldwide, and for each place «See on map» and «✨ Create itinerary».
  5. For a pre-written program use Itinerary library › Themed › Dark skies & stars.

What you read on the card

  • Possible badges: "Sky (Bortle scale) N" and 🌌 the designation (for example International Dark Sky), 🆓 "Free access" or 🎟 "With ticket".
  • In Events › Seasonal: «🌌 Tonight under the stars» with forecasted cloud cover, moon phase and ongoing meteor showers (10 peaking showers; "active" within 5 days of the peak).

Themed card filters: In the "Themed itineraries" card: "🆓 Free" and "🎟 With ticket".

Audio and photos

This theme does not have automatic audio (product decision): it has a card with a description and the «Audio» button that reads the text aloud when you ask. Photos are added after import and are not present for all places.

How the catalog is made

Moon calculations are local; cloud cover comes from MET Norway (NLOD / CC BY 4.0). The catalogue is curated by the editorial team and updated manually (there is no automatic update): an entry may sometimes be outdated. For this reason, every entry should be checked before you set off.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • No automatic audio for this theme.
  • The current catalogue lacks the Bortle scale, designation, and free access for all entries: the «by Bortle» sorting actually follows fame, and the Free / With ticket filters do not select anything significant.
  • On the map you see a sample of at most 400 places per tile: zoom in to see more.

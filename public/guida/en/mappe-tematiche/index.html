Thematic maps: all categories and all levels of WIP

The WIP map has two tools to see different things: the chips, which show places, and the levels, which draw routes, areas and data. Here you find each explained.

Addable chips with ＋ Altro

Tap ＋ Altro: the "Add to the bar" opens. Each row has a switch and an ⓘ button that explains what it contains. Turning a row on puts it in the bar and turns it on; turning it off removes it. Your choice remains saved on the device. All are off by default.

The eight themes (Themes chip)

The theme row appears only if Tematici is in the bar and on. "All" turns them all on; tapping a theme from "All" leaves only that one. Then add or remove one theme at a time; removing the last one returns to "All".

Map layers

The Livelli button is the circle with the layered icon, in the bottom left. If you have layers on it shows a dot with the number and their emojis next to it. Layers stack; each row has a check ✓ when it’s on and a spinning circle while loading. If "↗ zoom in to see them" appears you need to zoom in more. With two or more layers on there is "Turn off all (n)".

Tutorial: choose a theme and a layer together

  1. Tap ＋ More and turn on 🧭 Thematic.
  2. In the theme row tap, for example 🎨 Street art.
  3. Open Layers and turn on ☀️ Sun to choose the walk time.
  4. Tap a pin for the place card.
  5. To start over tap Turn off all in Layers and All in themes.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • On the bottom bar of the map there are the "Find nearby" and "Everything" buttons: their functionality will be documented after verification.
  • No layer for webcams, tides or the Blue Flag.
  • Limited traffic zones (ZTL) are no longer available.

Frequently asked questions

Why don’t I see a layer turned on?

You’re probably too far away: «zoom in to see them» appears. Zoom in on the area.

How do I remove everything?

Layers › "Turn off all".

Why doesn’t Thematic show the theme row?

It must be in the bar and turned on.

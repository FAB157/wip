Custom route from the map: step‑by‑step tutorial

From the map you can create a walking route that visits the places you choose, from any category. It costs 30 credits, includes 3 modifications and has no audio guide.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

In short

  • What it is: a route calculated by WIP that visits all selected places, with the WipNav navigator.
  • How much it costs: 30 credits, with confirmation before payment.
  • Modifications: up to 3 (add or remove places). Then you have to finish and create a new one.
  • Audio guide: not included. For audio guides you need the Tour with the Day Pass.

Tutorial: create your route

  1. On the map, tap \"Build a route\". The \"Custom route\" panel opens with the three options.
  2. Choose a path. With Personal go back to the map and tap the + on the pins you want; the panel shows \"{n} selected places\". With Gems around me wait for \"Looking for gems around you…\" and then remove the ones you’re not interested in. With Timed choose 1 hour, 2 hours or half a day.
  3. Check the price line: \"30 credits · up to 3 changes included · no audio guide\".
  4. Tap \"Create and start · 30 credits\". If you are not signed in, \"Sign in to create the route\" appears.
  5. \"Confirm the payment?\" appears: it reads the number of stops, the 3 changes and your balance. Tap \"Pay 30 credits and start\".
  6. The navigator starts immediately. Follow it like WipNav (see the navigator page).
  7. To change your mind, add or remove places from the map: the route recalculates from where you are. The counter says \"Changes used: n/3\".
  8. At the end, tap \"End route\" (asks for confirmation) or \"New route\". There is also \"All stops in Google Maps (car)\" to do it in the car.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • The custom route has no audio guide.
  • The maximum number of places per route is not documented here: the app indicates it when you reach it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the places after paying?

Yes, up to 3 changes; then you must create a new route.

If I finish the route, will I get the credits back?

No: credits already spent are not refunded.

Does it work for the car?

The WIP navigator is on foot; with the "All stops in Google Maps (car)" button you open the stops in Google Maps.

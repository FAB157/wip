Group tour (Live Tour): one guide, the whole group listens

With the Group Tour, one person acts as the guide: every audio guide they start plays at the same time on the phones of everyone in the group. Only the leader pays; followers listen for free, even without an account.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

Tutorial: create the tour (leader)

  1. Open Profile and tap the «Group Tour» tab.
  2. In the «Are you the Guide?» box, tap «Create Live Session».
  3. The Group PIN will appear: tell your friends or copy the link (it opens on wip.guide and joins automatically with the PIN).
  4. Below you can see who has joined: «In the group», with your name marked «Guide». Until someone joins, you will read «Only you connected — share the PIN!».
  5. Now use the app as usual: map, tour, free audio guide, museum visit. Every audio guide you start reaches the whole group.
  6. At the end of the tour, tap «End Session»: the PIN will stop working.

Tutorial: join the tour (follower)

  1. Open Profile › Group Tour (or the link sent by the leader).
  2. In the «Are you a Follower?» box, enter the PIN and confirm.
  3. «You are connected to the Tour» will appear. Keep your phone on: when the leader starts a place, the audio starts automatically.
  4. If your phone blocks automatic audio, «The leader unlocked a place — Tap to listen on the speaker» will appear: one tap and it starts.
  5. To exit, tap «Leave Group».
Good to know. Anyone joining as a guest can listen to the entire tour for free; by registering (for free) they receive 100 free credits for future trips.

In museums

  • If the leader has the Museum Visit, the group follows their visit: the room they are in, the chosen artwork, the story.
  • For followers, only the artwork chosen by the leader is free: other artworks require their own pass («Only the artwork chosen by the leader is free; others require the pass»).

The follower's voice

Followers hear the same audio guide as the leader, with the same voice (Nicky, Dante, or Duet) and in the leader's language. If that voice is not yet ready on the server, their phone reads the same text using the device's voice: followers never pay for the voice.

Messages you might see

MessageWhat does it mean
«Leader unreachable: not in the group for more than 30 seconds.»The leader lost connection or closed the app. Audio resumes when they return.

«Another device is already the leader of this tour.»The same tour is open as leader on two phones: use only one.

Group security

  • A session is found only by knowing the PIN: there is no public list of tours.
  • Only the leader transmits: no participant can send audio to the group.
  • The leader sees the list of connected participants.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • With the screen off, followers won't hear audio: followers' phones must stay on with WIP open.
  • Internet is required for everyone, for the entire tour.
  • There is no chat or voice channel: the leader doesn't speak “live”, the group hears the audio guides.
  • Guests joining do not always appear in the leader's participant list.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost for followers?

Nothing: only the leader pays, for the audio guides they start.

Does everyone need to have the app?

The wip.guide website in a browser or the app is enough; followers don't even need to register.

🛁 Hot springs & spas: the WIP catalog (2,757 places)

🛁 Hot springs & spas — Hot springs, onsen, hammam, thermal centers, historic baths, and spa towns around the world.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

What it is

Hot springs, onsen, hammam, thermal centers, historic baths, and spa towns around the world. The app catalog contains 2,757 of them, in 156 countries.

How to find it

  1. On the map tap ＋ More and turn on 🧭 Themed.
  2. In the theme row tap 🛁 Hot springs & spas. "All" turns them all on; tapping a theme from "All" leaves only that one.
  3. Zoom in on the area and tap a pin: the panel opens with a photo (if available), description, and the Guide, Navigate, and Audio buttons (reads the text aloud, upon request).
  4. Alternatively open Itinerary › 🧭 Themed travel › Hot springs & spas: list with search ("Search among N places: name, city, country…"), filters 📍 Near me (200 km radius) and 🌍 Worldwide, and for each place "See on map" and "✨ Create itinerary".
  5. For a pre-written program use the Itinerary library › Themed › Hot springs & spas.

What you read on the card

  • In the catalog: ♨️ water temperature, 💧 "Natural spring", 🏛 UNESCO, 🆓 "Free access" or 🎟 "With ticket", 🚪 "Open to visitors".
  • The card text indicates the type (spa town, non-swimmable spring to see, free pool, public bath, facility, thermal park, spa resort, historic bath, archaeological site), the water class (for example bromine-iodine-salt, bicarbonate, ferruginous) with "traditional" indications and a medical warning, amenities, how to get there, and warnings.

Themed card filters: In the "Themed itineraries" card: "🆓 Free" and "🎟 With ticket".

Audio and photos

This theme does not have automatic audio (product decision): it has a card with a description and the «Audio» button that reads the text aloud when you ask. Photos are added after import and are not present for all places.

How the catalog is made

Every entry must have proof of 'thermal nature' (law, temperature, Wikidata, source). Uncertain coordinates receive a wider radius and are marked as approximate. The catalog is curated by the editorial team and is updated by hand (there is no automatic update): an entry may no longer be up to date. For this reason, each card should be verified before leaving.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • No automatic audio for this theme.
  • On the map you see a sample of at most 400 places per tile: zoom in to see more.

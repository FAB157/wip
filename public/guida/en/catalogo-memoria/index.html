🕯️ Memory & house museums: the WIP catalog (147 places)

🕯️ Memory & house museums — Monumental cemeteries, war memorials, house museums, birth houses, tombs of personalities, memory sites and mausoleums.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

What it is

Monumental cemeteries, war memorials, house museums, birth houses, tombs of personalities, memory sites and mausoleums. The app's catalog contains 147, in 28 countries.

How to find it

  1. On the map tap ＋ More and turn on 🧭 Themed.
  2. In the themes row tap 🕯️ Memory & house museums. "All" turns them all on; tapping a theme from "All" leaves only that one.
  3. Zoom in on the area and tap a pin: the panel opens with a photo (if available), description, and the Guide, Navigate, and Audio buttons (reads the text aloud, upon request).
  4. Alternatively open Itinerary › 🧭 Thematic trips › Memory & house museums: list with search (“Cerca fra N luoghi: nome, città, paese…”), filters 📍 Near me (radius 200 km) and 🌍 Worldwide, and for each place "See on map" and "✨ Create itinerary".
  5. For an already written program use the Itinerary library › Thematic › Memory & house museums.

What you read on the card

  • The type, the city and the description; for almost all entries the character and their role (“🕯 person — role”).

Themed card filters: Only search by name, city, or country.

A selection of the best known, with a maximum of three per country. The complete list is in the app, under "Themed itineraries".

Westminster Abbey — tombs of Newton and DarwinLondon, United KingdomTomb of a personalityIn the nave, the monument to Isaac Newton; a few meters away the slab of Charles Darwin, buried here in 1882 almost against the wishes of his own family.

The Cavern ClubLiverpool, United KingdomMemory siteThe Mathew Street cellar where the Beatles played nearly 300 times between 1961 and 1963: daily live music and the wall of names at the entrance.

Audio and photos

This theme does not have automatic audio (product decision): it has a card with a description and the «Audio» button that reads the text aloud when you ask. Photos are added after import and are not present for all places.

How the catalog is made

It's the smallest vertical: house museums are the majority. In the Library the theme has a dedicated chip, distinct from the old "memory" theme. The catalog is curated by the editorial team and is updated manually (there is no automatic update): an entry may no longer be up to date. For this reason, each card must be checked before leaving.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • No automatic audio for this theme.
  • On the map you see a sample of at most 400 places per tile: zoom in to see more.

Frequently asked questions

How many places are there in "Memory & house museums"?

The curated catalog contains 147. More or less may appear on the map because the map reads the place archive.

Does it have an audio guide?

No: it has a card and an "Audio" button that reads the text aloud.

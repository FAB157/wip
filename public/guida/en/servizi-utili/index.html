Useful services and families: pharmacies, water fountains, charging, playgrounds

In an unfamiliar city the problem is often "where is the nearest pharmacy?". The Utility chip answers in two taps; the Families chip gathers playgrounds, zoos and aquariums.

Utility: what you can search

  1. Tap ＋ More and turn on 🛠️ Utility.
  2. Below the bar choose: Pharmacy, Hospital, Markets, Water Fountains, Train Station, Metro, Taxi, Highway (toll booth), EV Charging.
  3. Tap the nearest pin and Navigate.

The 🚰 Water Fountains, restrooms, benches level (from Layers) shows water points, toilets and benches within about 2 km of the map centre.

Families

The 🛝 Families chip includes: Playground, Amusements, Aquarium, Zoo.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • The parking spots do not have a dedicated filter.
  • There are no filters for airports, banks/ATMs, laundries, supermarkets nor services for disabled people or pets.
  • Public transport has no planner: there is the "Transit" link to Google Maps in itineraries.

Frequently asked questions

Can I find a parking spot?

There is no dedicated chip for parking.

Can I find charging stations?

Yes: among the Utility filters there is "EV Charging".

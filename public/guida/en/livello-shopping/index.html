🏬 Shopping: how the map layer works

🏬 Shopping («Where to go» group) — shopping streets and centers, department stores, outlets, souks.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

How to turn it on

  1. On the map, tap the circle with the layers («Map layers») at the bottom left.
  2. Find the group («Where to go», «How it is now» or «Background») and tap the layer row: the row colors and a ✓ appears (a spinner while loading).
  3. Close the panel: next to the button, the emojis of the active layers and a dot with the number remain. Tap them to reopen.
  4. To turn it off, tap the row again, or «Turn off all (n)».

What it shows on the map

  • Round pins with the emoji of the type: 🛍️ shopping streets · 🏬 department stores · 🏢 shopping centers · 🏷️ outlets · 🕌 souk · ✈️ duty free. Without grouping or legend.

Zoom visibility

From zoom 2 (extended from 09/26/2026); reloaded on every move, maximum 200 points per view.

What you see when you tap

  • Bubble with name, description, «website ↗» link and phone number, plus short text and photos loaded upon opening.

Where the data comes from

WIP service place archive (from open data).

Options and combinations

Options: None.

With other layers: It does not have a chip: it can only be turned on from Layers.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • No opening hours, no store brands: only the category.
  • The 200-point cap cuts off dense areas.

Frequently asked questions

Does the layer «Shopping» have a filter?

None.

Why can't I see it?

Check that you have turned the layer on and are close enough: if “zoom in to see them” appears, zoom in on the area.

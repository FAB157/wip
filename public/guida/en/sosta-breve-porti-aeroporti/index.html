Short stop: what to do with a few hours in a port or an airport

Do you have a cruise with a few hours on land or a long layover? The Short stop offers excursions with limited hours and always calculates the return: to the ship or to the gate.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

How to open

  1. Itinerary › Automatic Mode › 🛳✈️ Short stop panel.
  2. The \"Short stop\" screen opens: \"Excursions with limited hours: return to the ship or gate always guaranteed\".
  3. Search in \"Search port or airport worldwide…\"; filter All / 🛳 Ports / ✈️ Airports; choose the country from \"All countries\".
  4. Tap an entry: see \"To the centre\" and, for airports, the note on luggage storage.
  5. Choose the hours: Ground stop hours (ports) or Layover hours (airports), usually 4 h / 6 h / 8 h; 🛋 for \"stay in airport\".
  6. Read the amber banner with the return constraint (see below) and the numbered preview of the steps.
  7. Tap \"Use this itinerary ✨\": fill out the form (1 day, Tour on, Free off, return constraint as duration). Does not generate yet: you tap \"Generate\" (10 credits).
  8. \"See the ready itineraries for this stop\" opens the Library filtered on the city.

The return constraint

TypeDisplayed rule
PortReturn on board within N−1 hours from the start of the stop (1 hour margin: the ship does not wait).
AirportReturn to airport within N−2 hours (2 hours before the flight).
\"Stay in airport\"You stay in the airport: at the gate 2 hours before the flight.

What’s in the catalog

Approximately 175 entries curated by the editorial team (badge "✦ Editorial"): 127 ports, about 48 airports and 27 “stay in airport” options. If your port or airport isn’t listed, \"Generate with AI the guide for the port / airport\" (badge 🤖 AI) appears: AI consent and access are required. The cost of this guide isn’t shown in the app; the final itinerary generation costs 10 credits per day.

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • The catalog numbers are those stated in the app (approx.), not a database count.
  • The cost of the AI‑generated guide for a port not in the catalog isn’t indicated.

Frequently asked questions

If I’m wrong and return late?

WIP can’t guarantee the return: it calculates the margin but you always check the ship or flight times.

Is it free?

Consulting the catalog and previews is free; generating the itinerary costs the equivalent of one day (10 credits).

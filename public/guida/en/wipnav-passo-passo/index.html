WipNav: the foot navigation with voice, step by step

WipNav is the app’s foot navigator: it guides you with voice and, along the way, automatically starts the audio guides of the places you choose. It is free.

Translated from Italian. Screenshots, videos and examples show the app in Italian.

How to start

  1. Open a place’s card and tap Navigate (or the arrow in Radar, the green button on the map or ➤ Navigate on the alert card).
  2. In the sheet that opens, choose 🚶 On foot ("With WIP Nav: audio guides along the route"). If needed, tick ♿ Avoid stairs (strollers, reduced mobility). 🚗 In car opens Google Maps (on iPhone also Maps).
  3. In the WIP Nav sheet select the starting point: “From my location” or “Custom Address” (type at least 3 letters and choose from 5 suggestions).
  4. See “the places you’ll encounter along the way” (within 60 m of the route): they are all checked and the audio guide starts automatically only for the selected ones. Uncheck the ones you don’t want.
  5. Tap “Start navigation” (or Skip if you don’t want places along the way). The route is drawn in yellow with the flag 🏁.
Good to know. In itinerary mode the rows become “On foot, with the audio guide” and “By car, the whole day” (Google Maps with all stops, up to 10).

Voice, language and alerts

  • The phrase follows the app language (7 languages); street names are read in the country’s language.
  • Alert: “In N meters, …” at about 150 m (not under 60 m) and “turn now” at 30 m. Linked to the route, the alerts follow your speed (about 70 seconds before the turn). They are not configurable.
  • No alert for “continue straight”.
  • Vibration (app only): turn to the left = two pulses, right = one strong pulse; also on arrival.
  • Silence: there is no separate button for WipNav; use the round 🔊/🔇 button on the Guide key. The text remains on screen.
  • “Turn around”: at the start, if you are facing the opposite direction of the route, WipNav says it.

Off route, reroute, arrival

SituationWhat WipNav does
You are more than 45 m from the route for 2 consecutive detectionsIt reroutes automatically and says “Route recalculated” (20‑second pause between reroutes).
No networkContinue on the old route and try again after 20, 40, 80, 120 seconds.
Arrivals (within your arrival radius, default 30 m)It says “You have arrived at …”, then the teaser, then the audio guide starts and the card opens.
Route finished and destination less than 60 m away, or 45 seconds within 60 mArrival still declared.
Tap the XStops voice and dashboard and removes the track.

GPS readings worse than 80 m are not used.

Screen off

In the iPhone and Android app the navigator keeps announcing turns with the screen off (notification on Android, Live Activity on iPhone). In the browser the page stops. If you take a wrong turn with the screen off, the voice reports it but the recalculation occurs when you reopen the app. The screen stays on while you navigate, where the phone allows it. On Android you need precise location ("Settings › Apps › WIP › Location").

If something goes wrong

  • \"Activate GPS or choose a starting address\": location is missing.
  • \"Unable to calculate the route. Please try again.\": none of the routing sources responded, please try again.
  • With a custom starting address, recalculation and arrival remain suspended until you are on the route (within 60 m).

What WIP does not do (so you know what to expect)

  • WipNav is for walking only.
  • The warning distances (150 m and 30 m) cannot be changed.
  • There is no vibration on the web.
  • \"Transit\" is in the tour dashboard for long legs, not in the WipNav card.

Frequently asked questions

Does WipNav cost credits?

No, it’s free, even within a tour.

Can I change when it notifies me?

No: the warnings are automatic. You can change the arrival distance in GeoControl.

Does it work in a car?

No: for the car use Google Maps (or Maps on iPhone) from the "Navigate" sheet.
